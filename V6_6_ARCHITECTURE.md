# VOC Research World — V6.6

## Scientific Knowledge Hub & Article Reader

V6.6 upgrades the individual article experience into a structured scientific reader while preserving the V6.5 content architecture.

### Article reader
- Structured article metadata
- Abstract and section navigation
- Sticky contents/navigation on desktop
- Reading-time estimate
- Author and affiliation links
- Research-area and article-type metadata
- Keywords
- Research-project connection
- Publication record connection
- APA / Harvard / Vancouver citation tools
- References section
- Related research discovery
- Print / Save as PDF support

### Data relationship
`Author → Article → Project → Publication → References → Related Articles`

The content remains centralized in `data.js`, so a future database/CMS can replace the static data layer without redesigning the reader interface.
