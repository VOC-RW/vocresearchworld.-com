WHAT WE OFFER

Publication, QA advisory and mentorship

Three services, each grounded in the same lab discipline the rest of the site is built on.

01

Research publication

We review and publish research notes across biochemistry, laboratory science and industrial studies. Submissions are assessed on method and clarity, open to student and independent researchers alongside institutional ones.

02

Quality assurance advisory

We advise labs and small producers on QA/QC process design — sampling protocols, documentation and testing benchmarks aligned to NAFDAC, SON and RMRDC standards.

03

Research mentorship

One-on-one support for students preparing coursework projects, literature reviews or first submissions — from framing a research question to structuring the final write-up.

04

Industrial materials review

Assessment of raw material sourcing and substitution options for small manufacturers, drawing on industrial resource-inventory research methods.
