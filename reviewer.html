Reviewer responsibilities

  • Assess the scientific quality and relevance of each manuscript.
  • Provide specific, constructive feedback to the author.
  • Keep confidential editorial comments separate from author feedback.
  • Disclose potential conflicts of interest to the editor.
  • Submit recommendations without treating them as final editorial decisions.
