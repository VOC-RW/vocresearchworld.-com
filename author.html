From manuscript to publication

Understand the research publication pathway

Scientific publication involves more than uploading a document. A manuscript must meet the applicable requirements and undergo the relevant editorial process.

STAGE 01

Prepare your work

Develop your research question, use appropriate methods, analyze the evidence, and write a clear manuscript.

STAGE 02

Submit for consideration

Provide the required manuscript and submission information through the VOC submission process.

STAGE 03

Editorial assessment

The submission may be assessed and reviewed, with revisions requested where appropriate.

STAGE 04

Decision and publication

The editorial decision determines whether a manuscript is accepted, requires revision, or is declined. Accepted work may proceed to publication.

Our commitment to responsible research

Authors are expected to report findings honestly, cite sources accurately, acknowledge legitimate contributions, protect confidential information, and follow applicable ethical and laboratory safety requirements. Do not fabricate or manipulate data, plagiarize another person's work, or claim authorship without a legitimate contribution. Obtain appropriate supervision, permissions, and ethical approvals before conducting regulated research. AI tools, where used, should be used responsibly and disclosed when required by the applicable guidelines.
