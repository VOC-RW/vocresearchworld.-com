RESEARCH SUBMISSION

Submit your scientific work.

Send a manuscript or research proposal to the VOC Research World editorial team using our email-based submission workflow.

How submission works

Your manuscript details are prepared as an email to vocresearchworld@gmail.com with aolaniyan59@gmail.com copied. If your device supports file sharing, the selected manuscript can be attached through your email/share app. Otherwise, your email app will open and you can attach the manuscript manually.

Recommended: PDF. Accepted formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX or TXT. Maximum size for the browser sharing workflow: 10 MB.
