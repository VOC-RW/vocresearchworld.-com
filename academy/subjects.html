These are initial academic subject areas. Their detailed topic libraries, learning materials and teacher-led lessons will be developed progressively.

LEARNING LEVEL 01

Secondary School Learning

Strengthen foundational knowledge, understand classroom concepts and develop confidence in science and mathematics.

Planned focus: introductory Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics and General Science.

LEARNING LEVEL 02

College and Polytechnic Learning

Develop deeper subject knowledge and connect theoretical concepts with technical and practical applications.

Planned focus: applied sciences, laboratory techniques, computing, engineering foundations and technical subjects.

LEARNING LEVEL 03

University Learning

Explore advanced concepts, scientific reasoning, academic problem-solving and deeper subject understanding.

Planned focus: university-level life sciences, analytical sciences, instrumentation, data science and engineering subjects.

LEARNING LEVEL 04

Independent Learning

Learn beyond the classroom. Explore scientific ideas, develop new skills and strengthen your existing knowledge at your own pace.

YOUR LEARNING JOURNEY
