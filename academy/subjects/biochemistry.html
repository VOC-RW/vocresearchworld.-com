ABOUT THIS SUBJECT

What is Biochemistry?

Biochemistry is the branch of science that studies the chemical substances, molecular structures and chemical reactions occurring in living organisms.

It connects biology and chemistry to explain how cells obtain energy, how enzymes control reactions, how genetic information is expressed and how the body maintains its normal functions.

Your learning journey

Foundations of Chemistry → Biomolecules → Enzymes → Metabolism → Molecular Biology → Clinical Biochemistry

LEARNING OBJECTIVES

What you will understand

  • Explain the chemical composition and organisation of living cells.
  • Identify the structures and functions of major biomolecules.
  • Understand enzyme activity and factors affecting biochemical reactions.
  • Explain major metabolic pathways and energy production.
  • Understand the relationship between biochemical processes and human health.
  • Connect biochemical theory with laboratory analysis and research.
