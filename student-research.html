Your research workflow

From an idea to a scientific manuscript

Research requires a clear question, a suitable method, careful analysis, and honest reporting. These steps provide a starting framework; specific projects may require additional procedures.

STEP 01

Define the question

Identify a problem, review existing literature, and determine what you want to investigate.

STEP 02

Plan the method

Choose an appropriate design, identify required resources, and obtain necessary approvals before starting.

STEP 03

Analyze and write

Record genuine observations, analyze data appropriately, cite sources, and report limitations.

STEP 04

Prepare and submit

Check manuscript quality, authorship, references, ethics requirements, and the submission guidelines.

Research integrity comes first.

Scientific progress depends on honesty, accurate reporting, proper citation, responsible data handling, and respect for research participants and laboratory safety. Never fabricate results, plagiarize another person's work, or claim authorship without a legitimate contribution. Obtain required supervision and approvals before conducting experiments or collecting data.
